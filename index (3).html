How it works

Simple on purpose. Your team shouldn’t need training to open a window.

1
Step one

We fit your window

The retrofit device attaches to your existing sliding glass transaction window — no demolition, no replacement glass. The unit itself is compact — about 3.5″ wide by 6.5″ tall.

2
Step two

Press the button

Staff open or close the window with a single push. Smooth, quiet motion, every time.

3
Step three

It stays closed

Because closing takes no effort, privacy, hygiene, and climate control stop being a daily battle.
